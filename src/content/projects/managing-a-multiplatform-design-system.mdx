<Chapter title="Accessibility" id="accessibility">
  Early on, the design team ran an audit together with the accessibility experts at adesso. It revealed significant gaps and moved accessibility high up the roadmap. I pushed to invest the time in fixing the problems at the system level rather than feature by feature.

  We reworked the color palette to meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast in both light and dark mode and defined all necessary states for interactive components.

  I specified how designers annotate their flows so that engineers can more easily add screen reader labels and keyboard order.

<Chapter title="Reality check" id="reality-check">
  Adoption was slow. Engineers were busy with legacy code, and the roadmap favored new features.

  I worked around it: I ran sessions with some motivated engineers and built the token system alongside my feature work. Once I had access to AI tools, I could analyze the codebase myself, and results came much faster. The color tokens in production today are the result.

  Looking back, I wouldn't wait for permission to improve the system. I'd build the foundations alongside feature work from the start, and push to get it on the roadmap early.
</Chapter>
